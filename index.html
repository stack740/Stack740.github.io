01 / ABOUT

About me

Hello! I'm Mandy. I come from China and I'm currently studying in the CMCT programme at Hang Seng University of Hong Kong.

I love discovering new possibilities while learning, and I try to record my ideas in simple, clear ways. Most of what I know about music came from years of practice rather than talent — which is exactly why I trust slow, repeated work.

This site is where those two habits meet: the careful note-taking of a student and the listening ear of a musician. Nothing here is finished, and that's on purpose.

04 / MUSIC LOG

What I'm practising

A small practice tracker. Pick an instrument to see what I'm working on and the habit I'm trying to build.

Piano

This month's goal: play one piece from memory, without stopping, even when I make a mistake.

I still practise hands separately on anything new. It feels slower, but the piece holds together far better afterwards.

  • Scales & arpeggios — 10 minutes
  • Current piece, hands separately
  • Current piece, hands together, slowly
  • One bar I keep getting wrong

Flute

This month's goal: hold a steady tone through a long phrase without my pitch drifting sharp.

Tone is the whole instrument. I would rather play four beautiful long notes than rush through a page.

  • Long tones, low register
  • Long tones, high register
  • Tuning against a drone
  • Ensemble part — listening first

Voice

This month's goal: sing a full song and actually enjoy the performance instead of monitoring myself.

Recording myself was uncomfortable at first, but it is the only honest way to know what I actually sound like.

  • Breathing and posture
  • Warm-ups, top to bottom
  • One phrase, repeated
  • Full run-through, recorded
